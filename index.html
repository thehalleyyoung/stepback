Trace replay and its semantic boundaries

Stepback records agent runs as signed traces, identifies steps affected by substitutions, and reuses the recorded prefix. Agent adapters reconstruct state for live continuation. Our evidence describes where this works and where additional semantic information is required.

What the study establishes

Saved records cover 1,200 public trajectories and 5,943 classifications. Cumulative history dependencies produce suffix invalidation in those records. Local A/B controls show that invalidation hashes do not reconstruct changed message text. Signatures establish artifact integrity under the chosen keys, not semantic truth.
